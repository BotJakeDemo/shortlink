`zurdo analyze` (1.30.1) flags a `[shell:]` hint of the form `test "$(curl …)" = "…"` as a no-op, even though it compares real output. Pipe the command into an exact-line match instead: `[shell: curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code} %{redirect_url}\n' <url> | grep -qx '302 <target>']`. It expresses the same check, exits non-zero when the output differs (including against a dead server), and clears the lint.
